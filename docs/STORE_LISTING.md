## Short description (≤ 100 chars)
Offline two-way Indian Sign Language ⇄ speech translator. Preview: 263 everyday words.

## Description
SANKET helps a Deaf or mute person and a hearing person talk to each other on one Windows PC — at a shop, a bank counter, a clinic or an office.

• Sign → Voice: sign Indian Sign Language in front of the webcam. SANKET recognises each sign, shows the sentence in Hindi and English, and speaks it aloud.
• Voice → Sign: the hearing person holds Space and speaks Hindi or English. An on-screen avatar signs the reply in ISL, with large captions.
• 100% offline: no internet, no account, no data leaves your computer. Video and audio are never saved.
• Built for readability: large text, high-contrast theme, full keyboard control.
• Learn: a dictionary of all 263 signs with slow-motion playback.

This is a PREVIEW. SANKET understands 263 everyday isolated ISL words (the INCLUDE vocabulary). It is not a replacement for a qualified interpreter. Avatar signs are copied from real recordings by Deaf signers and community verification is in progress — use "This sign looks wrong" to report a sign.

Sign data: INCLUDE dataset by AI4Bharat / IIT Madras (CC BY 4.0), signed by Deaf students of St. Louis School for the Deaf, Adyar, Chennai.

## Capability justification (webcam / microphone)
Webcam: reads sign language gestures; frames are processed in memory only. Microphone: converts the hearing person's speech to text on-device while the Speak button is held.

## Notes for certification
Fully offline desktop app (runFullTrust). Requires a webcam for sign recognition and a microphone for speech. To test without signing: Settings → open the Dictionary (Shabdkosh) and play any sign; hold Space on the Conversation screen and say "namaste".
