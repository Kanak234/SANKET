# SANKET — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: Kanak Prabhakar · Contact: via https://github.com/Kanak234/Sankat/issues

**Short version: SANKET works completely offline. Nothing you sign, say or type ever leaves your computer.**

## What SANKET uses
- **Camera** — to see sign language. Each frame is turned into body/hand points in memory and discarded immediately. Video is never recorded or saved.
- **Microphone** — only while you hold the Speak button (max 15 seconds), to turn speech into text on your device. Audio is never saved.

## What SANKET stores on your computer
- Your settings (theme, text size, camera, languages) in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SANKET\settings.json`.
- Optional "This sign looks wrong" notes in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SANKET\sign_reports.jsonl`.
- Optional **Contribute** recordings: only if you switch Contribute on AND tick the consent box. These contain hand/face/body **points only (no video, no audio)**, saved in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SANKET\contrib\`. SANKET never sends them anywhere; you decide whether to share them yourself.
- The conversation transcript exists only while the app is open and is erased when you close it.

## What SANKET does NOT do
No account, no login, no internet connection, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies, no third-party services, no sale or sharing of data. SANKET does not request the internet capability in its Windows package.

## Children
SANKET collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data
Uninstall SANKET and delete the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SANKET`.

## Laws
Written to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and IT Act 2000, and the EU GDPR — SANKET processes no personal data off-device.
